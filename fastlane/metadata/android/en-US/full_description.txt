Inklus is a free and open source handwriting app for tablets with a stylus (and desktop).

• Pressure-sensitive pens, pencil, highlighter, calligraphy and brush
• Palm rejection and stylus-only mode, S-Pen button as eraser
• Infinite or fixed pages, Cornell, weekly/monthly planners, graph paper, music staff
• Annotate PDFs, insert images, text boxes, layers, lasso, shapes, ruler and protractor
• Search inside your handwriting (on-device recognition, optional)
• Export to PNG, PDF, SVG and PowerPoint; open .inklus files anywhere
• Offline-first: optional encrypted Google Drive backup, no tracking, no ads
• Free marketplace of templates and colour palettes (including colour-blind safe ones)
• Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German

A "lite" build without handwriting recognition is ~17 MB smaller.
